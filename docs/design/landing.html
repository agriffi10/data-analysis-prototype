Everything you need to manage your files

A focused workspace — no clutter, just your files where you expect them.

Fast, resilient uploads

Drag and drop files up to several gigabytes. Large files switch to multipart upload automatically, with live progress for every file.

A space that's yours

Browse, organize into folders, rename, move, and download. Your space is private to you — no one else can see or touch it.

Connect to shared data

Link the shared project prefixes you have access to and work with them alongside your own files, all from one place.

Isolation enforced at the infrastructure layer

Authentication runs on AWS Cognito and every user is scoped to their own storage by AWS IAM — not by application code. Get started in under a minute.
